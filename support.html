Common questions

How do subscriptions work? Plus and Pro are optional plans billed monthly or yearly through your Apple ID. They auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the app's Plan screen and tap "Restore purchases."

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete account.
